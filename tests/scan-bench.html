Scanner test bench

Runs the claim tool's own reading on every sample in Documents/WFC-Tools/scan-tests/samples (on this PC only, never on GitHub) and scores it against labels.json. Five ways: pc (this PC only), tool-sonnet and tool-opus (this PC, with Claude when unsure, as the claim tool works), claude-sonnet and claude-opus (Claude alone). Each Claude reading costs a few sen through the company connection.





New samples


To add a sample: put the file in scan-tests/samples, press Start labels for new samples, check every field in the box below (fix anything wrong; list fields you cannot read in "unsure"), then paste its entry into labels.json.
